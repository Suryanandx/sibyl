    Finding your way around

    The rail on the left is the same on every screen. It has a few groups. A group with more than one part shows them as tabs at the top of its screens.

    Rail Tabs Addresses
    Inbox (bell, top) /inbox
    Chat /, /c/<id>
    Agents /agents, /agents/<id>/computer
    Meetings /meetings, /meetings/<id>, /call/<id>
    Spaces Slides · Media /library/slides, /media
    Library Knowledge · Skills · Plugins · Integrations /knowledge, /skills, /skills/plugins, /integrations
    Automations Routines · Monitors /routines, /monitors
    Settings (bottom) Admin sections inside Settings /settings/<section>, /admin/<section>

    At the bottom of the rail you also find the Getting started ring (until you finish or dismiss it), the light/dark switch and the connection dot.

    • Inbox. The bell opens the inbox: approvals waiting for you and what routines, monitors and agents did. The badge counts what is new.
    • Admin. Organization owners and admins see an Admin heading in the Settings list (Organization, Users, Single sign-on, and so on). Admin sections keep their own addresses, /admin/<section>.
    • Only what you can use. Groups and tabs you have no access to are hidden. A viewer, for example, has no Integrations tab. A group with no tabs left disappears, and a group opens on the first tab you can use.
    • Addresses. Every address above works as a link and with Back and Forward. The rail highlights the group of the screen you are on.

    #On a phone

    There is no rail. In the chat, the menu button opens a short list of the same groups (Agents, Meetings, Spaces, Library, Automations, Settings) above your conversations. The bell sits in the chat's top bar. Tabs scroll sideways under a screen's title. The back arrow on a screen returns to the chat.

    #For developers

    The groups and tabs are defined once in apps/web/lib/nav.ts; placeOf in apps/web/lib/routes.ts names the group of an address. ScreenFrame shows the tabs of the current group on every screen, so a new screen whose address is listed as a tab gets them without extra code.
